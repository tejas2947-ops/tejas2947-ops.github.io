✨ What gentle-dot Does

gentle-dot sits quietly on your desktop and acts like a helpful companion. You tell it things once, and it remembers them. You ask for something, and it does its best to take care of it using the tools already available on your Mac. Most assistant tools forget everything the moment you close the window. gentle-dot works the other way around. It keeps a gentle, persistent memory of the details you care about, so you do not have to repeat yourself every morning. Think of it as a patient helper who actually pays attention.
